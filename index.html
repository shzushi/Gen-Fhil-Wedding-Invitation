Our Story

Where It All Began

It all started when he saw me on a jeepney on my way to school. He said that my long, curly hair and pointed nose caught his attention, and he instantly thought that I would be the one he would marry someday. From that day on, he started pursuing me, and one day he finally gathered the courage to message me on Facebook. At first, I ignored him because I thought we were too young to be in a relationship. However, he continued to pursue me and never gave up. He insisted that he would wait. Eventually, we found ourselves getting to know each other better, and we agreed to wait for the right time.

Learning to Wait

It may sound corny and ordinary, but we did not notice how quickly time passed as we enjoyed our college years, our friendships, and our so-called "waiting relationship." He graduated, and I was there for him. Then I graduated, and he was there for me. Unconsciously, we both remained committed to waiting for each other. We thought that finishing our degrees meant we had finally reached the right time, but it did not. It was only the true beginning of faithful waiting. There were many more priorities in our careers and personal lives that we needed to face. Yet the Lord helped us wait, support one another, and respect each other's time to grow.

Love Through the Distance

One of the greatest tests of our faith and patience was when he entered the six-month foundational training course under the National Police Training Institute. For four months, we endured every painful minute and hour without any communication. All we had were our prayers, love, faith, hope, and trust that the Lord would help us through the sacrifice of time and distance.

A Love That Held On

After the fourth month, he found a secret way to reach me. Through what they called a "take life," he would write a letter on a piece of paper, crumple it, and throw it to a kind civilian outside the training center. The handwritten letter would then be photographed and sent to me. It was such a dramatic experience, yet it was also incredibly sweet and filled with genuine love and sacrifice. When he completed his training and became an official member of the Philippine National Police (PNP), we thought that the waiting was finally over. However, he was assigned to the PNP National Headquarters in Camp Crame, Quezon City. It was then that we realized the Lord was not yet finished strengthening our faith and patience.

Faith in the Waiting

All we could do was wait and trust Him. Then, by His grace, another five years passed. Those five years were filled with love, faith, hope, patience, helping our families, preparing ourselves for the future, and establishing our careers while holding on to our promise of waiting.

The Day We Said Yes

On January 1, 2026, the Lord blessed us and gave Fhil the courage to propose to me. At last, we were able to experience the fruit of all our waiting and sacrifice.

The Promise Fulfilled

To this day, we can only testify to the countless lessons and experiences the Lord has given us, molding us into the people we need to be for the joyful and beautiful future ahead of us. Every month and every year since 2016, for eleven years, we have celebrated not the day I said yes to his courtship, but the day we chose to wait for each other until we were blessed with the gift of time and the opportunity to face the future together, no longer separated by distance.
